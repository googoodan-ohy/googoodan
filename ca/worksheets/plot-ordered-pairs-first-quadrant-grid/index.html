Teaching Notes

Use the worked example and teaching sequence below to explain the reasoning before independent practice.

Before You Begin

  • Read the horizontal and vertical axes.

Teaching Steps for Parents and Teachers

  1. Begin at the origin.
  2. Move horizontally by the first coordinate.
  3. Move vertically by the second coordinate and mark the point.

Worked Examples

Plot (3, 2) in the first quadrant.

  1. Move 3 units right from (0, 0).
  2. Move 2 units up.

Answer: The point is 3 units right and 2 units up from the origin.

Common Mistakes and How to Help

The first coordinate is horizontal; do not reverse the pair.

Read back the x-coordinate first, then the y-coordinate.

Questions to Check Understanding

  • Read back the x-coordinate first, then the y-coordinate.
  • What in the numbers or diagram supports your answer?

Practice Advice

Work through the example together. Ask the learner to explain the first two problems, then try three independently. Review the first incorrect step before assigning more practice.
