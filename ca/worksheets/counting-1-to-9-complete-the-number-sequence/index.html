Counting 1–9 Worksheet — Complete the Number Sequence

Free math practice for home or the classroom. Print this Counting 1–9 Worksheet — Complete the Number Sequence and its answer key on Letter paper, one page each.

Use New Numbers to make another set. Choose Worksheet or Answer Key, then use the worksheet’s Print button.

Teaching Notes

Focus: Completing a counting sequence from 1 to 9. Use the neighboring numbers to identify the missing number.

Before You Begin

  • Say number names in order.

Teaching Steps for Parents and Teachers

  1. Read the numbers in order.
  2. Find the step between consecutive numbers.
  3. Continue that pattern through the blank and check the sequence.

Worked Examples

Complete the sequence: 2, 3, □, 5

  1. The numbers increase by 1 each time.
  2. After 3 comes 4, and after 4 comes 5.

Answer: 4

Common Mistakes and How to Help

Skipping a number or counting the starting number as the next step.

Say the sequence aloud one number at a time. What comes immediately after 3?

Questions to Check Understanding

  • How does each number change?
  • Which number comes just before the blank?
  • Does your answer keep the sequence in order?

Practice Advice

Work through the example together. Ask the learner to explain the first two problems, then try three independently. Review the first incorrect step before assigning more practice.

Next Practice

  • Use New Numbers to practice the same skill with different values.
  • If the learner needs help, return to the example and model each step before trying again.
