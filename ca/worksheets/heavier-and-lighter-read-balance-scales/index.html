Heavier and Lighter Worksheet: Read Balance Scales

Free math practice for home or the classroom. Print this Heavier and Lighter Worksheet: Read Balance Scales and its answer key on Letter paper, one page each.

Use New Numbers to make another set. Choose Worksheet or Answer Key, then use the worksheet’s Print button.

Teaching Notes

Use the worked example and teaching sequence below to explain the reasoning before independent practice.

Before You Begin

  • Recognize heavier and lighter.

Teaching Steps for Parents and Teachers

  1. Compare the stated weights in the same unit, or read a balance.
  2. On a balance, the lower side is heavier.
  3. Use the requested comparison word.

Worked Examples

A weighs 4 units and B weighs 7 of the same units. Which is lighter?

  1. Compare 4 and 7.
  2. 4 < 7.

Answer: A

Common Mistakes and How to Help

Size alone does not tell which object is heavier.

Are both weights expressed in the same unit?

Questions to Check Understanding

  • Are both weights expressed in the same unit?
  • What in the numbers or diagram supports your answer?

Practice Advice

Work through the example together. Ask the learner to explain the first two problems, then try three independently. Review the first incorrect step before assigning more practice.
