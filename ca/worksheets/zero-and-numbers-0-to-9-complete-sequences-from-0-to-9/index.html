Zero and Numbers 0–9 Worksheet — Complete Sequences from 0 to 9

Free math practice for home or the classroom. Print this Zero and Numbers 0–9 Worksheet — Complete Sequences from 0 to 9 and its answer key on Letter paper, one page each.

Use New Numbers to make another set. Choose Worksheet or Answer Key, then use the worksheet’s Print button.

Teaching Notes

Focus: Completing number sequences from 0 to 9. Identify the counting pattern and use it to fill the missing position.

Before You Begin

  • Count forward and backward.

Teaching Steps for Parents and Teachers

  1. Read the sequence from left to right.
  2. Determine how the numbers change from one position to the next.
  3. Use that pattern to fill the blank and check both neighbors.

Worked Examples

Complete the sequence: 0, 1, □, 3

  1. The sequence increases by 1 each time.
  2. The number after 1 is 2, and 2 is followed by 3.

Answer: 2

Common Mistakes and How to Help

Starting the count at 1 when the sequence begins at 0.

Read every shown number from the beginning. What comes after 1?

Questions to Check Understanding

  • What is the pattern from one number to the next?
  • What number comes after 1?
  • Does the completed sequence include every number in order?

Practice Advice

Work through the example together. Ask the learner to explain the first two problems, then try three independently. Review the first incorrect step before assigning more practice.

Next Practice

  • If the learner needs help, return to the example and model each step before trying again.
