Teaching Notes

Focus: Comparing Numbers. Use the worked example and teaching sequence below to explain the reasoning before independent practice.

Before You Begin

  • Read each number by place value.

Teaching Steps for Parents and Teachers

  1. Compare the greatest place first.
  2. If those digits match, compare the next place.
  3. For an ordered list, choose the smallest remaining number each time.

Worked Examples

Order 4, 7, and 2 from least to greatest.

  1. 2 is less than 4 and 7.
  2. 4 is less than 7.

Answer: 2 < 4 < 7

Common Mistakes and How to Help

The open side of an inequality sign faces the greater value.

Read the completed comparison aloud and check every pair.

Questions to Check Understanding

  • Read the completed comparison aloud and check every pair.
  • Which step explains your answer?
  • Can you apply the same reasoning when the numbers change?

Practice Advice

Work through the example together. Ask the learner to explain the first two problems, then try three independently. Review the first incorrect step before assigning more practice.

Next Practice

  • If the learner needs help, return to the example and model each step before trying again.
