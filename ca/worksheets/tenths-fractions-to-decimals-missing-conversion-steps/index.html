Teaching Notes

Focus: Fractions with Denominator 10 as Decimals. Use the worked example and teaching sequence below to explain the reasoning before independent practice.

Before You Begin

  • Recognize tenths as equal parts of one whole.

Teaching Steps for Parents and Teachers

  1. Read the denominator: 10 names tenths.
  2. Place the numerator in the tenths place.
  3. Check that the fraction and decimal name the same amount.

Worked Examples

Write 7/10 as a decimal.

  1. The denominator 10 means tenths.
  2. 7 tenths is 0.7.

Answer: 0.7

Common Mistakes and How to Help

7/10 is seven tenths, not seven hundredths.

Use the denominator to identify the place: 10 means tenths.

Questions to Check Understanding

  • How many tenths does 7/10 name?
  • Why does 0.7 have the same value as 7/10?

Practice Advice

Work through the example together. Ask the learner to explain the first two problems, then try three independently. Review the first incorrect step before assigning more practice.
