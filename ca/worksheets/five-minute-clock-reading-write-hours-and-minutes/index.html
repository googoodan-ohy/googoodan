Five-Minute Clock Reading: Write Hours and Minutes

Five-Minute Clock Reading: Write Hours and Minutes: a printable math practice sheet with an answer key, ideal for printable math practice. Make fresh sets as often as you like and print on Letter paper.

Use New Numbers to make another set. Choose Worksheet or Answer Key, then use the worksheet’s Print button.

Teaching Notes

Use the worked example and teaching sequence below to explain the reasoning before independent practice.

Before You Begin

  • Know that one hour has 60 minutes.

Teaching Steps for Parents and Teachers

  1. Read the minute hand using five minutes per numbered mark.
  2. Read the hour that the hour hand has passed.
  3. Write hours and minutes, using two digits for minutes.

Worked Examples

The minute hand points to 6 and the hour hand is between 3 and 4. What time is it?

  1. 6 × 5 = 30 minutes.
  2. The hour hand has passed 3 but not 4.

Answer: 3:30

Common Mistakes and How to Help

At half past, the hour hand is between numbers; do not round up to the next hour.

Does the hour hand position agree with the minute reading?

Questions to Check Understanding

  • Does the hour hand position agree with the minute reading?
  • What in the numbers or diagram supports your answer?

Practice Advice

Work through the example together. Ask the learner to explain the first two problems, then try three independently. Review the first incorrect step before assigning more practice.

Next Practice
