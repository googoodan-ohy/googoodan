Printing from a phone?

iPhone / iPad: Safari → Share → Print. Use an AirPrint printer on the same Wi-Fi network.

Android: Chrome → More (⋮) → Share → Print. Select a printer or Save as PDF if available.

If the Print button does not open a dialog, use your browser’s menu. Options depend on your device and printer.

Print: Portrait · Scale 100% · Default margins · Headers and footers off.
