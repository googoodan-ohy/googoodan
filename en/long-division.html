Use the worked answer key

Divide, multiply, subtract, and bring down the next digit. For example, 156 ÷ 3 = 52: divide 15 by 3, subtract 15, bring down 6, and divide 6 by 3. Check a result by multiplying the quotient by the divisor and adding any remainder. The remainder must be smaller than the divisor.

  • Multiplication review
  • Subtraction with regrouping

Where this practice fits

Grade 4 learners work with one-digit divisors using place value and other strategies. Grade 5 extends division to two-digit divisors, and grade 6 includes fluency with the standard algorithm. These sheets practice the written algorithm; use models and explanations alongside them when that is the method taught in class.
