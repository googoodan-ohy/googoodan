Grades 2–3: name and model equal parts

Pictures help children understand that a fraction describes equal parts of one whole or a set. Ask students to explain the denominator as the number of equal parts and the numerator as the number being counted. Number-line work then connects fractions to numbers with a location and size.

Grades 3–5: equivalent and comparable fractions

Equivalent-fraction practice helps students see that different numerators and denominators can name the same value. Use models first, then compare by common denominators, common numerators, benchmark fractions, or cross products when appropriate.

Grades 4–6: fraction operations

For addition and subtraction, identify whether the denominators are already alike before finding a common denominator. For multiplication, connect factors to scaling and models. For division, keep the order of the dividend and divisor clear and check the quotient by multiplying.
