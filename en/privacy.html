Privacy in this preview

Updated September 6, 2026.

The English worksheet application generates problems in your browser. It has no account registration, student data forms, or live advertising scripts. Optional Google Analytics integration is installed but remains inactive until a measurement ID is configured on the production host and you grant permission. Your analytics preference is saved in browser storage; after permission, Google Analytics may use analytics cookies. The Name and Date fields are blank lines intended for writing on paper.

The server hosting the website may process ordinary request information such as IP addresses and requested pages. Links to external curriculum references open third-party sites with their own policies.

Advertisement boxes currently display placeholders. This notice must be reviewed before adding live advertising, analytics, accounts, or other data-collection features.
