Privacy Policy

How the current English edition handles information.

Last updated: September 6, 2026.

Worksheet information

Problems and answers are generated in your browser. The English edition does not offer student accounts, online answer submission, comments, or file uploads. Name and Date on the worksheet are blank lines for writing on paper, not online input fields.

Links and printing

Worksheet links can include the selected grade, activity, and a numeric Set identifier used to recreate questions. That identifier describes a problem set and is not intended as a student identifier. Printing is handled by your browser, device, and chosen printer or PDF destination.

Hosting information

Hosting and network providers may process request information such as IP addresses, browser details, requested pages, and timestamps to deliver and secure the site. Provider retention and processing depend on the hosting service. We have not yet published a provider-specific retention schedule.

Advertising and analytics

Advertisement boxes in this edition are placeholders; they do not load Google advertising code. Google Analytics is configured and loads only on production hosts after your permission. It measures visits, worksheet use, printing clicks, and PDF preparation. You can decline or change your choice using Analytics preferences. Basic page-load totals are counted separately using temporary request IDs and security rate limits; daily aggregate totals remain.

Children and families

The activities are intended for elementary learning with help from parents and teachers. We do not ask children to submit personal information through the worksheet interface. This does not mean hosting or third-party services can never process technical information. Live advertising or analytics requires a separate review of the actual data practices before use.

Contact and requests

For privacy questions or requests, email ohy0973@gmail.com. Please avoid sending children’s personal information unless it is necessary to explain your request. Messages sent to this address are processed by the email provider and used to respond to your inquiry.

External websites and changes

External curriculum references and other linked websites have their own privacy practices. We will update this notice when the service’s data practices change.

Country and region preferences

The country and region you select are stored in this browser to remember your choice across pages. You can clear them by deleting site data. They do not identify a student, and selecting a region does not automatically enable region-specific curriculum mapping.

For the separate Korean community and its posts, photos, and older sign-in features, see the Korean privacy notice.
