Missing Side Length Worksheets: model, solve and check

Free printable missing side length worksheets. Use given measurements to find an unknown length, show your reasoning and check with answer keys.

Grade 4. These worksheets reverse a familiar measurement calculation: instead of finding an area or volume from lengths, use the given result to find a missing dimension. Students should already understand the relevant area, volume or surface-area formula.

Choose an activity

  • Rectangle: missing length

How to use these worksheets

Read each question carefully and underline the measurements you know. Draw a rectangle, triangle, parallelogram, prism or cube as needed. Label the known lengths and mark the unknown dimension with a question mark. The workspace is intentionally blank so the learner can build a model.

Write an equation before calculating. After finding the missing length, substitute it into the original formula. A length is measured in centimeters, an area in square centimeters, and a volume in cubic centimeters. Keeping track of these units helps distinguish the result from the information supplied.

Print a worksheet and answer key

Choose a focused practice type or a mixed set. Each worksheet has four problems with room for a drawing and calculation. Use New problems for another number set. Open Answer key to view the matching answers and explanations. The print controls let you print the worksheet, solutions, or both on A4 or US Letter paper. Teaching notes

Connect the rectangle area formula to multiplication with an unknown factor. All questions use metric units already present in the source activities; this is geometry reasoning, not unit-conversion practice.

Explain which operation undoes the original calculation.

Show a labeled model and verify your answer.
