Choose a comfortable starting point

Use within-10 practice when a child still counts every object. Move to sums and differences within 20 when the smaller facts feel familiar. Counters, fingers and drawings are useful ways to explain an answer; there is no need to race the clock.

Build number sense alongside calculation

Comparing quantities and understanding tens and ones help children make sense of written equations. For missing-number practice, ask which quantity is unknown before the child starts calculating. The word-problem option uses addition and subtraction with small whole numbers.
