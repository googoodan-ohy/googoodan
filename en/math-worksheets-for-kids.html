Print a matching problem sheet and answer key

  1. Choose a skill and preview the worksheet.
  2. Select the pages you need using the worksheet and answer-key checkboxes.
  3. Use Print, or save as PDF in the browser’s print dialog. Choose A4 or Letter and check the preview.
  4. Use New numbers when you want a different set. Print its new answer key too.

Worksheets and answer keys are free. Parents and teachers may print them for home practice or classroom lessons. Selling the worksheets or redistributing them for a fee is not permitted.
