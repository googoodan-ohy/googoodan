5th Grade Math Worksheets

Free printable math worksheets for fifth grade, with answer keys. Fifth grade is the year most children either secure fractions or quietly fall behind in them, so the fraction sets here are deliberately generous.

What fifth grade covers

Fifth grade covers fractions with unlike denominators, multiplication of fractions, decimal addition and subtraction, and long division with two-digit divisors.

When a fifth grader cannot add unlike fractions, the fraction work is rarely the problem. Nine times out of ten the child cannot find a common multiple quickly, which means the times tables are not secure. Going back to third grade multiplication for a fortnight fixes more fifth grade fraction work than any amount of fraction practice.

How to use these sheets

  1. Choose the skill your child is working on.
  2. Preview the worksheet and the answer key.
  3. Print it, or press the number-change button for a completely new set of questions.

There is no limit and no sign-up. If a child needs the same skill twenty times, print it twenty times. That is the whole reason this site exists: a workbook runs out, and this does not.

Teachers and parents outside the United States sometimes search for free printable maths worksheets rather than math worksheets. The sheets are the same, and the questions suit any curriculum that teaches the four operations in this order.
