Choose the Operation Word Problems Worksheets: free printable activity

Free printable choose the operation word problems for grades 1–2. Decide whether to add or subtract, solve fresh stories, and print matching answer keys.

Grades 1–2 · 1.OA.A.1; 2.OA.A.1. Students decide which operation represents the relationship in each story. The choices include total, starting-amount, and change-unknown situations with familiar school contexts.

What students do

Underline the known quantities and circle what the question asks. Compare the two offered equations, choose the one that matches the story, and then calculate. Select New problems whenever another version is useful. The activity keeps the same learning goal while the existing worksheet engine supplies a fresh set of numbers, pictures, or story details.

A practical teaching routine

Do not teach a single clue-word rule. Ask whether quantities are being joined, separated, or compared, and identify which quantity is unknown before choosing addition or subtraction. Begin with one example together, let the student complete the remaining questions independently, and ask for a short explanation of one answer. The explanation helps distinguish a lucky guess from an understood strategy.
