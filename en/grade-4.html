4th Grade Math Worksheets

Free printable math worksheets for fourth grade, each with an answer key and each one different from the last. Fourth grade is where arithmetic becomes multi-step, and multi-step work needs repetition to become reliable.

What fourth grade covers

Fourth grade covers multi-digit multiplication, long division with remainders, and the beginning of serious fraction work: adding and subtracting fractions that share a denominator.

Long division fails more often from misaligned columns than from faulty arithmetic. A child who writes the quotient one column off will get every question wrong while doing the mathematics correctly. Squared paper, or simply insisting on straight columns, fixes more fourth grade division than extra explanation does.

How to use these sheets

  1. Choose the skill your child is working on.
  2. Preview the worksheet and the answer key.
  3. Print it, or press the number-change button for a completely new set of questions.

There is no limit and no sign-up. If a child needs the same skill twenty times, print it twenty times. That is the whole reason this site exists: a workbook runs out, and this does not.
