3rd Grade Math Worksheets

Free printable math worksheets for third grade. Multiplication and division arrive this year, and both reward volume of practice more than any other topic in elementary school. Every sheet here regenerates with new numbers.

What third grade covers

Third grade brings the times tables, division as the inverse of multiplication, and three-digit addition and subtraction. Fractions appear at the end of the year as parts of a whole.

Children who learned the times tables by chanting them in order often cannot answer out of order. Ask for 7 x 6 straight away, with no run-up, and you will see quickly whether a table is truly known. The 6, 7 and 8 tables take the longest for almost every child. Division stalls for the same reason: a child who does not know 7 x 8 cannot see that 56 divided by 7 is 8.

How to use these sheets

  1. Choose the skill your child is working on.
  2. Preview the worksheet and the answer key.
  3. Print it, or press the number-change button for a completely new set of questions.

There is no limit and no sign-up. If a child needs the same skill twenty times, print it twenty times. That is the whole reason this site exists: a workbook runs out, and this does not.

Teachers and parents outside the United States sometimes search for free printable maths worksheets rather than math worksheets. The sheets are the same, and the questions suit any curriculum that teaches the four operations in this order.
