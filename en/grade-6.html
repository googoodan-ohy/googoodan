6th Grade Math Worksheets - Free Printable

Free printable math worksheets for sixth grade, each with an answer key. Sixth grade is the last year of pure arithmetic before algebra takes over, and gaps left here show up immediately in middle school.

What sixth grade covers

Sixth grade covers division of fractions, multiplication and division of decimals, and the four operations with positive and negative integers.

Dividing by a fraction is the point where children stop trusting arithmetic, because the answer gets bigger. Showing that 3 divided by one half asks how many halves fit into 3 usually settles it in one lesson. Negative numbers cause a separate problem: children apply the sign rules for multiplication to addition. Keeping the two sets of rules apart for a while helps.

How to use these sheets

  1. Choose the skill your child is working on.
  2. Preview the worksheet and the answer key.
  3. Print it, or press the number-change button for a completely new set of questions.

There is no limit and no sign-up. If a child needs the same skill twenty times, print it twenty times. That is the whole reason this site exists: a workbook runs out, and this does not.

Teachers and parents outside the United States sometimes search for free printable maths worksheets rather than math worksheets. The sheets are the same, and the questions suit any curriculum that teaches the four operations in this order.
