Subtracting Three Numbers Worksheets: free printable activity

Free printable subtracting three numbers worksheets for first grade. Subtract within 10 from left to right, make fresh sets, and print matching answers.

Grade 1 · 1.OA.C.6. Every equation begins with a number from 6 through 9 and subtracts two small amounts. All intermediate and final values stay nonnegative and within 10.

What students do

Cover the third number, solve the first subtraction, then subtract the last number from that result. Record the final difference and check it by repeating the two steps. Select New problems whenever another version is useful. The activity keeps the same learning goal while the existing worksheet engine supplies a fresh set of numbers, pictures, or story details.

A practical teaching routine

Have the student say the intermediate result aloud. This prevents treating both smaller numbers as one number and reinforces that equal-priority subtraction is worked from left to right. Begin with one example together, let the student complete the remaining questions independently, and ask for a short explanation of one answer. The explanation helps distinguish a lucky guess from an understood strategy.
