Volume of Composite Rectangular Prisms

Teach composite volume by separating a solid into nonoverlapping parts.

Teaching notes for parents and teachers

How to teach it

Identify rectangular prisms, label their dimensions, and find each volume. Add the volumes after checking that every part is included once.

Example to work through together

Use the model below as you explain the example. Have your student point to the quantities as you discuss them.

One part measures 4 × 3 × 2 units, so its volume is 24 cubic units. Another measures 2 × 3 × 1, so its volume is 6. Together they have volume 30 cubic units.

Ask your student

Do any of our parts overlap?

If your student needs help

If a region is counted twice, color the separate prisms and match each to one calculation.
