Arithmetic Patterns

Help your student test a pattern rule across every given step.

Teaching notes for parents and teachers

How to teach it

Compare consecutive terms and try the proposed operation on each pair. Discuss why a rule must fit more than the first change.

Example to work through together

Use the model below as you explain the example. Have your student point to the quantities as you discuss them.

For 4, 8, 12, 16, add 4 each time. The next terms are 20 and 24. In 3, 6, 12, 24, multiply by 2 instead.

Ask your student

Does your rule work from the third term to the fourth?

If your student needs help

If it fails later, have the student revise the rule before extending the sequence.
