Counting Coins

Teach coin values before asking students to count a mixed collection.

Teaching notes for parents and teachers

How to teach it

Identify each U.S. coin, then model counting on by its value. Begin with the larger values when that makes the count easier.

Example to work through together

Use the model below as you explain the example. Have your student point to the quantities as you discuss them.

A quarter, two dimes, and three pennies total 25 + 10 + 10 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 48 cents.

Ask your student

Why does a dime add ten to our total even though it is one coin?

If your student needs help

If the student counts coins instead of cents, label each coin's value and add those labels.
