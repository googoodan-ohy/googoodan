Grade 4 Math Worksheets for Parents and Teachers

Choose a skill for the child you teach. Read the teaching notes, work through an example together, then print a worksheet and a separate answer key. Grade labels are a guide; use earlier skills when your student needs more support.

  • Adding Fractions Worksheets: Like Denominators

    Use fraction strips to show why the denominator stays the same when adding like fractions.

  • Subtracting Fractions With Like Denominators Worksheets

    Use like-sized fraction pieces to explain subtraction with like denominators.

  • Multiplication & Division Word Problems

    Teach your student to identify the groups, group size, and total before calculating.

  • Multi-Digit Addition & Subtraction Word Problems

    Teach large-number word problems by separating the situation from the arithmetic.

  • 2-Digit Multiplication Word Problems

    Connect two-digit multiplication to rectangular area.

  • 4-Digit Division Word Problems

    Teach students to interpret a remainder in the context of the story.

  • 4-Digit by 1-Digit Multiplication Word Problems

    Use an area model to explain multiplication with a four-digit factor.

  • Like-Denominator Fraction Word Problems

    Keep the unit whole visible when teaching fraction word problems.

  • Multiplying Fractions by Whole Numbers Worksheets

    Show multiplication by a whole number as repeated copies of a fraction.

  • Factors and Factor Pairs Word Problems

    Teach factor pairs by arranging a fixed collection into complete rows.

  • Division Word Problems: Vans Needed

    Teach students to decide whether a remainder requires an additional group.

  • Missing Fraction Part Word Problems

    Keep the same reference whole when teaching missing fraction amounts.

  • Equal Groups Division Word Problems

    Help your student distinguish equal sharing from finding the number of groups.

  • Multiplicative Comparison Word Problems

    Distinguish times-as-many comparisons from additive comparisons.

  • Comparing Numbers Worksheets: Large Whole Numbers

    Teach large-number comparison with aligned place-value columns.

  • Comparing Fractions Worksheets: Unlike Denominators

    Teach unlike-fraction comparison using equivalent fractions or a benchmark.

  • Fractions and Decimals Worksheets: Tenths and Hundredths

    Connect tenths, hundredths, and decimals with a hundred grid.

  • Factors and Multiples Worksheets: Prime and Composite Numbers

    Use factor pairs to distinguish prime and composite numbers.

  • Lines of Symmetry Worksheets

    Teach symmetry by checking whether a fold makes the entire shape match.

  • Geometry Worksheets: Lines, Rays, and Line Segments

    Use endpoints and arrows to distinguish lines, rays, and segments.

  • Measurement Conversion Worksheets

    Teach unit conversion as renaming an unchanged measurement.

  • Angles Worksheets: Measuring and Drawing Angles

    Model how to position and read a protractor.

  • Comparing Decimals Worksheets Through Hundredths

    Teach decimal comparison through hundredths using equal place values.
