Teaching resources for parents and teachers

Grade 2 Math Worksheets for Parents and Teachers

Choose a skill for the child you teach. Read the teaching notes, work through an example together, then print a worksheet and a separate answer key. Grade labels are a guide; use earlier skills when your student needs more support.

  • 2-Digit Addition Worksheets Without Regrouping

    Use this worksheet to teach place value before introducing regrouping.

  • Telling Time Worksheets to 5 Minutes

    Use a clock with movable hands to teach time to the nearest five minutes.

  • Skip Counting Worksheets by 5, 10, and 100

    Teach skip counting as repeated equal jumps from the given starting number.

  • Picture Graph Worksheets

    Show your student how a graph key connects each symbol to a count.

  • 2-Digit Subtraction Without Regrouping Worksheets

    Use tens and ones to teach subtraction without regrouping.

  • 2-Digit Subtraction With Regrouping Worksheets

    Show your student that regrouping changes how a number is represented, not its value.

  • 2-Digit Addition With Regrouping Worksheets

    Teach regrouping in addition as an exchange of ten ones for one ten.

  • Addition & Subtraction Word Problems Within 20

    Help your student connect an addition or subtraction story to an action.

  • Word Problems Within 100

    Separate understanding the story from carrying out the calculation.

  • Word Problems Within 1,000

    Help your student recognize familiar story structures when the numbers get larger.

  • Adding Four 2-Digit Numbers Word Problems

    Teach flexible grouping when adding four two-digit numbers.

  • Two-Step Addition and Subtraction Word Problems

    Teach students to track two changes in one story.

  • Length Word Problems

    Use a consistent scale to explain two changes in a length.

  • Missing Part Word Problems Within 100

    Teach a missing part as the amount needed to complete a known whole.

  • Difference Word Problems Within 100

    Use comparison models to teach differences with larger numbers.

  • Comparing Numbers Worksheets: 3-Digit Numbers

    Teach three-digit comparison from the greatest place value.

  • Place Value Worksheets: 3-Digit Expanded Form

    Use expanded form to show the value represented by each digit.

  • Fractions Worksheets: Halves, Thirds, and Fourths

    Teach equal area as the essential feature of fractional parts.

  • Odd and Even Numbers Worksheets Within 20

    Use pairs to explain odd and even numbers.

  • Line Plot Worksheets: Reading Measurements

    Teach students to read a line plot as repeated measurements.

  • 10 or 100 More and Less Worksheets

    Teach changes of ten or one hundred using place-value units.

  • Number Names Worksheets: 3-Digit Numbers

    Connect number names to place value, including unspoken zeros.

  • Measurement Worksheets: Estimating and Measuring a Book

    Use estimation to give a measurement context before reaching for a ruler.

  • Shape Attributes Worksheets: Sides, Corners, and Faces

    Teach the attributes of flat shapes and solids with objects students can handle.
