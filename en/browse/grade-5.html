Grade 5 Math Worksheets for Parents and Teachers

Choose a skill for the child you teach. Read the teaching notes, work through an example together, then print a worksheet and a separate answer key. Grade labels are a guide; use earlier skills when your student needs more support.

  • Adding Decimals Worksheets to Hundredths

    Teach decimal addition by connecting each regrouping step to place value.

  • Decimal Addition & Subtraction Word Problems

    Help your student translate the cable-length story before working with decimals.

  • Subtracting Decimals to Hundredths Worksheets

    Teach decimal subtraction by modeling each regrouping step.

  • Multiplying Decimals: Tenths by Tenths Worksheets

    Use an area model to explain why tenths times tenths gives hundredths.

  • Dividing Decimals: Tenths by Tenths Worksheets

    Teach decimal division by scaling both quantities together.

  • Fraction Multiplication: Area Word Problems

    Connect a fractional side length to the area of a rectangle.

  • Two Fractions: Area Word Problems

    Use overlapping partitions to teach area with two fractional side lengths.

  • Unlike-Denominator Fraction Word Problems

    Teach common denominators as a way to work with equal-sized pieces.

  • Whole Number ÷ Unit Fraction Word Problems

    Use fraction strips to show how many small pieces fit into a whole-number length.

  • 4-Digit by 2-Digit Division Word Problems

    Teach division with two-digit divisors using estimates and labeled groups.

  • 3-Digit by 2-Digit Multiplication Word Problems

    Use partial areas to explain three-digit by two-digit multiplication.

  • Word Problems with Parentheses

    Use the contents of one box to explain parentheses.

  • Fraction Word Problems: Smaller Recipes

    Explain scaling a recipe by referring to the original ingredient amount.

  • Missing Fraction Parts: Unlike Denominators Worksheets

    Use equivalent fractions to find a missing part with unlike denominators.

  • Missing Decimal Part Word Problems

    Teach students to identify the missing amount before calculating with decimals.

  • Decimal Division Word Problems: Full and Partial Groups

    Use a measurement model to explain a quotient that includes part of a group.

  • Decimal Division Word Problems: Decimal Group Sizes

    Explain division by a decimal using a clearly labeled group size.

  • Multiplying Fractions Word Problems: Scaling Up

    Use one full copy and an extra part to explain multiplication by a fraction greater than one.

  • Decimal Scaling Word Problems

    Use ribbon lengths to explain decimal scale factors.

  • Decimal Place Value Worksheets: Identify the Digit

    Use a place-value chart to distinguish a digit from its value.

  • Comparing Decimals Worksheets Through Thousandths

    Teach decimal comparison by matching place values.

  • Simplifying Fractions Worksheets

    Explain simplifying as renaming the same fraction.

  • Common Denominator Worksheets

    Teach common denominators with matching wholes.

  • Volume Worksheets: Unit Cubes and Layers

    Teach volume by building and counting equal layers.

  • Coordinate Plane Worksheets: Reading Ordered Pairs

    Model the order of coordinates before introducing faster plotting.

  • Rounding Decimals Worksheets

    Connect decimal rounding to a number line before teaching the shortcut.

  • Powers of 10 Worksheets: Multiplying and Dividing

    Teach powers of ten as changes in place value.

  • Measurement Conversion Worksheets: Larger Units

    Explain why a larger unit requires a smaller count for the same measurement.
