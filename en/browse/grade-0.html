Kindergarten Math Worksheets for Parents and Teachers

Choose a skill for the child you teach. Read the teaching notes, work through an example together, then print a worksheet and a separate answer key. Grade labels are a guide; use earlier skills when your student needs more support.

  • Kindergarten Addition and Subtraction Worksheets Within 5

    Introduce addition and subtraction within five through actions with objects.

  • Kindergarten Addition and Subtraction Word Problems Within 5

    Read these short stories aloud and let your student act them out.

  • Count and Write Worksheets for Kindergarten

    Teach one-to-one counting by matching one spoken number to one picture.

  • Comparing Groups Worksheets for Kindergarten

    Use matching pairs to compare groups without relying on spacing.

  • Count and Circle Worksheets for Kindergarten – Free Printable Activities

    Teach selecting a given number from a larger collection.

  • Number Word Matching Worksheets for Kindergarten – Free Printable

    Keep the math accessible while introducing number words.

  • Ordering Numbers Worksheets for Kindergarten – Smallest to Largest

    Use movable cards or objects to teach ordering three numbers.

  • Before and After Numbers Worksheets for Kindergarten – Free Printable

    Teach before and after as one step in the counting sequence.

  • Missing Numbers on a Number Line Worksheets for Kindergarten

    Help your student use the labels already shown on a number line.

  • Ten Frame Worksheets: Making 10 for Kindergarten

    Use a ten-frame to show the missing part of ten.

  • Make 10 Missing Addend Worksheets for Kindergarten

    Connect a missing-addend equation to a model of ten.

  • Longer and Shorter Worksheets for Kindergarten

    Teach length comparison by lining up the starting points.

  • Sort and Count Shapes Worksheets for Kindergarten

    Teach sorting by one named shape attribute at a time.

  • 2D Shape Recognition Worksheets for Kindergarten

    Teach shape names through sides and corners rather than orientation.

  • Counting Sides Worksheets for Kindergarten

    Use tracing to help your student count each straight side once.

  • Repeating Shape Patterns Worksheets for Kindergarten

    Teach repeating patterns by identifying the whole repeating unit.

  • Missing Part Number Bonds Worksheets Within 9

    Teach a number bond as two parts of one pictured whole.

  • Ordinal Number Worksheets for Kindergarten — First to Eighth

    Distinguish an object's position from the number of objects.

  • Kindergarten Addition and Subtraction Word Problems Within 10

    Extend familiar joining and taking-away stories to quantities within ten.
