Teaching resources for parents and teachers

Grade 3 Math Worksheets for Parents and Teachers

Choose a skill for the child you teach. Read the teaching notes, work through an example together, then print a worksheet and a separate answer key. Grade labels are a guide; use earlier skills when your student needs more support.

  • Multiplication Worksheets: Equal Groups and Facts

    Teach multiplication with equal groups before asking your student to recall facts.

  • Quadrilateral Worksheets: Shape Classification

    Help your student classify shapes by their properties rather than their appearance.

  • Division Within 100 — No Remainders Worksheets

    Teach both meanings of division: equal sharing and making equal groups.

  • Multiplication & Division Word Problems

    Teach your student to identify the groups, group size, and total before calculating.

  • 3-Digit Addition and Subtraction Worksheets Within 1,000

    Use place-value models to teach addition and subtraction within 1,000.

  • Word Problems Within 1,000

    Help your student recognize familiar story structures when the numbers get larger.

  • Multiplying by Multiples of 10 Word Problems

    Connect multiplication facts to groups of ten.

  • Two-Step Multiplication and Subtraction Word Problems

    Help your student use an intermediate product in a two-step problem.

  • Liters and Grams Word Problems

    Distinguish the number of containers from the amount in each container.

  • Missing Part Word Problems Within 100

    Teach a missing part as the amount needed to complete a known whole.

  • Equal Groups Division Word Problems

    Help your student distinguish equal sharing from finding the number of groups.

  • Difference Word Problems Within 100

    Use comparison models to teach differences with larger numbers.

  • Comparing Fractions Worksheets: Like Denominators

    Teach fraction comparison by keeping the whole and the piece size the same.

  • Identifying Fractions Worksheets: Shaded Shapes

    Help your student distinguish the whole, all equal parts, and shaded parts.

  • Fractions on a Number Line Worksheets

    Teach fractions on a number line by identifying the unit interval first.

  • Perimeter Worksheets: Rectangles

    Teach perimeter as one complete trip around a boundary.

  • Area Worksheets: Counting Square Units

    Use unit squares to distinguish area from perimeter.

  • Rounding Worksheets to the Nearest 10 and 100

    Teach rounding by comparing distances to neighboring multiples.

  • Missing Number Worksheets: Factors and Dividends

    Teach students to locate the unknown before choosing a related fact.

  • Elapsed Time Word Problems

    Distinguish a clock time from an amount of elapsed time.

  • Line Plot Worksheets: Fraction Measurements

    Teach fraction line plots by separating the scale from the frequency.
