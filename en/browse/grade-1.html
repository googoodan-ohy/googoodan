Teaching resources for parents and teachers

Grade 1 Math Worksheets for Parents and Teachers

Choose a skill for the child you teach. Read the teaching notes, work through an example together, then print a worksheet and a separate answer key. Grade labels are a guide; use earlier skills when your student needs more support.

  • Addition Worksheets Within 20

    Help your student move from counting every object to counting on or making ten.

  • Subtraction Within 10 Worksheets

    Introduce subtraction by showing what is taken away and what remains.

  • Missing Addends Within 20 Worksheets

    Teach the blank as an unknown part of a total.

  • Addition & Subtraction Word Problems Within 20

    Help your student connect an addition or subtraction story to an action.

  • Addition and Subtraction Word Problems: Unknown Starting Number

    Help your student work backward from an ending total to an unknown starting amount.

  • More and Fewer Word Problems

    Use matching rows of counters to teach more-than and fewer-than comparisons.

  • Word Problems With Extra Information

    Teach your student to select information based on the question.

  • Money Word Problems: Finding Cents Spent

    Use this worksheet to teach an unknown amount spent, rather than coin identification.

  • Choose an Equation Word Problems

    Teach your student to choose an equation by identifying what is unknown.

  • Bar Model Word Problems

    Use bar models to make the relationship between a whole and its parts visible.

  • Same Numbers, Different Word Problems

    Help your student see why the same numbers can require different operations.

  • Explain Your Thinking Word Problems

    Use these problems to discuss reasoning as well as answers.

  • Find and Fix the Mistake Word Problems

    Teach students to check a proposed solution against the question.

  • Write Your Own Question Word Problems

    Help your student write a question that can be answered from the story.

  • Adding and Subtracting Tens Word Problems

    Teach addition and subtraction of tens by counting groups of ten.

  • 2-Digit Plus 1-Digit Addition Word Problems

    Help your student add ones to a two-digit number using a strategy that fits.

  • Difference Word Problems Within 20

    Teach a difference by matching two collections.

  • How Many More? Word Problems

    Show how counting on finds the amount needed to reach a target.

  • Place Value Worksheets: Tens and Ones Expanded Form

    Teach a ten as a group of ten ones.

  • Telling Time Worksheets: Hours and Half Hours

    Teach hours and half hours using both clock hands.

  • Comparing Numbers Worksheets: 2-Digit Numbers

    Teach two-digit comparison by comparing tens before ones.

  • 10 More and 10 Less Worksheets

    Show how adding or subtracting ten changes a number's groups.

  • Comparing Lengths Worksheets: Ordering 3 Objects

    Teach length comparison with aligned starting points.

  • Addition Equations Worksheets: True or False

    Use true-or-false equations to teach the meaning of the equal sign.

  • Shape Worksheets: Building and Drawing Shapes

    Teach shape composition with pieces students can move.
