Measurement Worksheets and Teaching Notes

Choose a skill for the child you teach. Read the teaching notes, work through an example together, then print a worksheet and a separate answer key. Grade labels are a guide; use earlier skills when your student needs more support.

  • Telling Time Worksheets to 5 Minutes

    Use a clock with movable hands to teach time to the nearest five minutes.

  • Length Word Problems

    Use a consistent scale to explain two changes in a length.

  • Liters and Grams Word Problems

    Distinguish the number of containers from the amount in each container.

  • Telling Time Worksheets: Hours and Half Hours

    Teach hours and half hours using both clock hands.

  • Perimeter Worksheets: Rectangles

    Teach perimeter as one complete trip around a boundary.

  • Area Worksheets: Counting Square Units

    Use unit squares to distinguish area from perimeter.

  • Volume Worksheets: Unit Cubes and Layers

    Teach volume by building and counting equal layers.

  • Comparing Lengths Worksheets: Ordering 3 Objects

    Teach length comparison with aligned starting points.

  • Elapsed Time Word Problems

    Distinguish a clock time from an amount of elapsed time.

  • Measurement Conversion Worksheets

    Teach unit conversion as renaming an unchanged measurement.

  • Angles Worksheets: Measuring and Drawing Angles

    Model how to position and read a protractor.

  • Measurement Worksheets: Estimating and Measuring a Book

    Use estimation to give a measurement context before reaching for a ruler.

  • Measurement Conversion Worksheets: Larger Units

    Explain why a larger unit requires a smaller count for the same measurement.

  • Longer and Shorter Worksheets for Kindergarten

    Teach length comparison by lining up the starting points.
