Teaching resources for parents and teachers

Geometry Worksheets and Teaching Notes

Choose a skill for the child you teach. Read the teaching notes, work through an example together, then print a worksheet and a separate answer key. Grade labels are a guide; use earlier skills when your student needs more support.

  • Quadrilateral Worksheets: Shape Classification

    Help your student classify shapes by their properties rather than their appearance.

  • Coordinate Plane Worksheets: Reading Ordered Pairs

    Model the order of coordinates before introducing faster plotting.

  • Fractions Worksheets: Halves, Thirds, and Fourths

    Teach equal area as the essential feature of fractional parts.

  • Lines of Symmetry Worksheets

    Teach symmetry by checking whether a fold makes the entire shape match.

  • Geometry Worksheets: Lines, Rays, and Line Segments

    Use endpoints and arrows to distinguish lines, rays, and segments.

  • Shape Worksheets: Building and Drawing Shapes

    Teach shape composition with pieces students can move.

  • Shape Attributes Worksheets: Sides, Corners, and Faces

    Teach the attributes of flat shapes and solids with objects students can handle.

  • Sort and Count Shapes Worksheets for Kindergarten

    Teach sorting by one named shape attribute at a time.

  • 2D Shape Recognition Worksheets for Kindergarten

    Teach shape names through sides and corners rather than orientation.

  • Counting Sides Worksheets for Kindergarten

    Use tracing to help your student count each straight side once.
