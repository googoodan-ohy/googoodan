## Scope and print settings

The collection spans the Common Core content domains in Grades 1–5. A worksheet practices selected aspects of its referenced standards; it is not a claim that one sheet assesses every subpart or proves mastery. Teachers should also observe discussion, mathematical explanations, practical measurement, and construction tasks. Open activities include checking guidance.

Source: https://www.thecorestandards.org/wp-content/uploads/Math_Standards.pdf (Grade 1–5 sections). Standards are not a mandated textbook sequence; states and schools vary.

All grades share the 190 mm content width, 250 mm minimum sheet height, bottom rule and googoodan.com/en brand. Runtime print fitting uses the answer layout and removes questions when necessary, keeping at least one question in each activity. Use portrait, 100% scale, default margins, headers and footers off.

Checks: node en/check-grades.cjs. grade-print-report.json records PDF page counts for A4 and Letter, worksheets and answer keys.
