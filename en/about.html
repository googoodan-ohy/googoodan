About Googoodan

Less setup. More practice.

Googoodan helps families and teachers choose a math worksheet, see the questions immediately, and print a matching worksheet or answer key.

Find your next activity

Browse by topic for arithmetic practice, or choose Kindergarten through Grade 5 for illustrated math activities. Grade collections are organized around Common Core content domains. State and school curricula vary, and a worksheet does not assess every part of a standard.

Kindergarten activities · Grade 1–5 math journals · Browse by topic

Made for paper

Choose Worksheet or Answer key, then Print. New problems creates another set. For the grade journals, question counts may adjust to fit the printed page. Use portrait orientation, A4 or Letter paper, 100% scale, default margins, and turn off browser headers and footers. Navigation and advertisement placeholders do not print.

Independent learning resources

Googoodan is an independent resource and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Common Core State Standards Initiative. An adult should check drawing, explanation, and hands-on activities.
