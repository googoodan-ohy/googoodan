Before You Begin

  • Recognize right angles and parallel sides.

Teaching Steps for Parents and Teachers

  1. A quadrilateral has four sides.
  2. Use parallel sides, equal lengths, and right angles to test each family definition.
  3. A shape may belong to more than one family.

Worked Examples

Is a square a rectangle?

  1. A rectangle has four right angles.
  2. A square has four right angles as well as four equal sides.

Answer: Yes

Common Mistakes and How to Help

Do not assume shape families are mutually exclusive.

Questions to Check Understanding
