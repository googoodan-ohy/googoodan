Teaching Notes

Before You Begin

  • Identify the requested place.

Teaching Steps for Parents and Teachers

  1. Find the digit in the requested place.
  2. Look one place to the right: 5 or more rounds up; less than 5 rounds down.
  3. Replace lower whole-number places with zeros, or remove unneeded decimal places.

Worked Examples

Round 347 to the nearest ten.

  1. The tens digit is 4.
  2. The ones digit is 7, so round up to the next ten.

Answer: 350

Common Mistakes and How to Help

Do not round repeatedly from the last digit; inspect the digit immediately right of the requested place.

Is 347 closer to 340 or 350?

Questions to Check Understanding

  • Is 347 closer to 340 or 350?
  • What in the numbers or diagram supports your answer?

Practice Advice

Work through the example together. Ask the learner to explain the first two problems, then try three independently. Review the first incorrect step before assigning more practice.
