Teaching Notes

Practice focus: decimal worksheet basic. Focus: Reading and Writing Tenths. Use the worked example and teaching sequence below to explain the reasoning before independent practice.

Before You Begin

  • Recognize tenths as equal parts of one whole.

Teaching Steps for Parents and Teachers

  1. Identify the number of tenths.
  2. Write the digit in the tenths place.
  3. Read the decimal as a number of tenths.

Worked Examples

Write seven tenths as a decimal.

  1. Tenths are the first place to the right of the decimal point.
  2. Write 7 in the tenths place.

Answer: 0.7

Common Mistakes and How to Help

0.7 is seven tenths; 0.07 is seven hundredths.

Point to the first place after the decimal point for tenths.

Questions to Check Understanding

  • Which place names tenths?
  • How do you read 0.7 in words?

Practice Advice

Work through the example together. Ask the learner to explain the first two problems, then try three independently. Review the first incorrect step before assigning more practice.

Next Practice
