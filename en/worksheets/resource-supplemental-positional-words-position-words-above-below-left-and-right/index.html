Teaching Notes

Use the worked example and teaching sequence below to explain the reasoning before independent practice.

Before You Begin

  • Recognize a reference object.

Teaching Steps for Parents and Teachers

  1. Identify the object named as the reference.
  2. Compare the other object with it.
  3. Use above, below, left, or right from the viewpoint shown.

Worked Examples

A star is directly above a circle. Where is the circle relative to the star?

  1. The star is higher.
  2. The circle is on the lower side of the star.

Answer: Below the star

Common Mistakes and How to Help

Position words depend on the reference object.

Which object is the reference in this question?

Questions to Check Understanding

  • Which object is the reference in this question?
  • What in the numbers or diagram supports your answer?

Practice Advice

Work through the example together. Ask the learner to explain the first two problems, then try three independently. Review the first incorrect step before assigning more practice.
