Before You Begin

  • Read the question and identify the skill.

Teaching Steps for Parents and Teachers

  1. For counting, touch each object once.
  2. For comparing, check the requested quantity or attribute.
  3. For shapes, use sides and corners rather than color or orientation.

Worked Examples

Count ● ● ● ● and compare the total with 6.

  1. There are 4 dots.
  2. 4 is less than 6.

Answer: 4 < 6

Common Mistakes and How to Help

Choose a method that matches the question rather than guessing from appearance.

What in the picture or numbers proves your answer?

Questions to Check Understanding

  • What in the picture or numbers proves your answer?
  • What in the numbers or diagram supports your answer?

Practice Advice

Work through the example together. Ask the learner to explain the first two problems, then try three independently. Review the first incorrect step before assigning more practice.

Next Practice

  • If the learner needs help, return to the example and model each step before trying again.
