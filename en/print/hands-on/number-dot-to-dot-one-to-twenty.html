What children do

Connect numbered dots from 1 to 20 in counting order. The task stays consistent across the page, while New problems changes the numbers or pictures for another practice set.

How the activity supports learning

Keep the pencil on the sequence, naming each number and checking the teen numbers carefully. Ask the child to describe one answer in words. A short explanation helps show whether the model and the math make sense together.
