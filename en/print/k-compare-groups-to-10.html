What children practice on this worksheet

Compare Groups to 10 gives kindergarten learners a focused way to explore more, fewer, equal. The activities support K.CC.C.6–7 and use small steps, pictures, drawing, or simple number models suited to an early learner.

Each generated sheet has three short sections: Explore and discover, Think and try, and Show what you know. This profile includes:

  • Find the group with more
  • Find the group with fewer
  • Compare equal groups

Use the printable in a short lesson

Read each direction aloud and let the child point, move counters, draw, or explain before writing. Complete one section at a time. Use New problems for another set with the same learning goal. Open the matching answer key to check fixed answers; drawing and open-response tasks include a sample or a clear checking note.

This worksheet is free to print for home or classroom use and is formatted for both US Letter and A4 paper. Select the worksheet, the answer key, or both before printing or saving a PDF.
