What second grade covers

Second grade is the year of regrouping. Children add and subtract two-digit numbers, carrying and borrowing across the tens column, and meet multiplication for the first time as repeated addition.

Two mistakes account for most wrong answers this year. In addition, children write the carried digit and then forget to add it. In subtraction, they borrow from the tens column but forget to reduce it by one. Both are habits, not misunderstandings, and both disappear with steady repetition of the same layout.

How to use these sheets

  1. Choose the skill your child is working on.

There is no limit and no sign-up. If a child needs the same skill twenty times, print it twenty times. That is the whole reason this site exists: a workbook runs out, and this does not.
