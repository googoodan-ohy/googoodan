Free printable cut and paste math worksheets for kindergarten

Practice counting, number words, number order, and partners for 10 on one mixed activity sheet. This page makes a real worksheet in the browser, so New problems creates another set instead of showing a fixed download.

Kindergarten · K.CC.A-B; K.OA.A.4. The worksheet and its matching answer key are free to print for home, classroom, tutoring, or math centers.

What children do

  • Count a picture group and paste its numeral.
  • Match written number words to numerals.
  • Order number cards from least to greatest.
  • Find and paste the missing partner that makes 10.

Use the mixed page when you want a short review of several early math skills. Each new set changes the pictures and numbers while keeping the same four-part routine.

Print the activity and answer key

Choose Worksheet for the student page and Answer key to preview the same pictures, cards, and numbers with answers. In Print selection, select either page or both. The print layout fits US Letter and A4 paper, and the browser print dialog can save the pages as a PDF.

Scissor and paste routine

Let an adult help with scissors when needed. Solve each task before gluing so a card can still be moved. The dotted borders identify the cut-out cards, and the larger dashed boxes are the spaces where answers belong. After checking, paste the cards and ask the learner to explain one match or number pattern.
