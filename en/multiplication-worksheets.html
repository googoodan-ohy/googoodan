Grades 4–5: multiply larger numbers

Place-value understanding supports every step of multi-digit multiplication. Start with one-digit factors and a consistent regrouping condition, then mix regrouping patterns and introduce two-digit factors. Estimation gives students a quick way to judge whether a product is reasonable.

Multiply fractions and decimals

After whole-number multiplication is secure, connect the same operation to fractions and decimals. Fraction models show why multiplying by a value less than one makes a product smaller. With decimals, estimate first and use place value to position the decimal point.
