Choose a day, or scan the QR code at the bottom of each practice page in the book.

Answers are numbered in the same order as the problems on each page (left to right, top to bottom). In story problems where the child fills all three boxes, the two parts of an addition may be written in either order (2 + 3 = 5 or 3 + 2 = 5). For “draw” tasks, any drawing with the right number of dots is correct. For “circle” tasks the key shows the letter or word to circle.
