Unit 8: Equivalent and Comparing Fractions

Answers are numbered in the same order as the problems on each page (left to right, top to bottom). In multiplication and addition, factors or addends written in the other order are also correct (3 × 4 = 12 or 4 × 3 = 12). An equivalent fraction (like 2/4 for 1/2) is also correct when a fraction is asked for. For shape names, the key gives the best name; a square is also a rectangle and a rhombus. Estimates in “About how many?” use numbers rounded to the nearest hundred.
