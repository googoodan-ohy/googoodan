Answers are numbered in the same order as the problems on each page (left to right, top to bottom). In word problems the two factors may be written in either order (6 × 47 or 47 × 6). Remainders are written as “857 ÷ 4 = 214 R 1”; write R 0 when nothing is left. In “Break apart” and area-model problems the parts are given, so answers should match the key. Numbers of 1000 or more may be written with or without a comma (2400 or 2,400).
