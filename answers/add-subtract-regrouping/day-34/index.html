Unit 4: Adding With Regrouping

Answers are numbered in the same order as the problems on each page (left to right, top to bottom). In column problems, the key lists the small regrouping numbers first (left to right), then the answer; children may show regrouping in other ways (crossing out, writing above) — the answer is what matters. On “open” columns with no small boxes, any correct regrouping marks are fine. In word problems, the two numbers in an addition may be written in either order, and a “how many more” or missing-part problem may be written as an addition with a missing number (for example 26 + 15 = 41) instead of a subtraction.
