Unit 9: Time to the Minute

Answers are numbered in the same order as the problems on each page (left to right, top to bottom). Clock answers may be written like 7:05 (a leading zero on the hour, such as 07:05, is also fine). For “Draw the hands,” check that the short hour hand sits between the two numbers shown in the key (or on the number for o’clock) and the long minute hand points where the key says. Money may be written in cents (135¢) or dollars ($1.35) unless the page asks for one way. Words like Tuesday or May may be abbreviated.
