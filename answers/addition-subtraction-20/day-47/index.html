Unit 6: Teen Numbers and Counting On to 20

Answers are numbered in the same order as the problems on each page (left to right, top to bottom). In addition, the order of the parts does not matter: 3 + 5 and 5 + 3 are both correct. In word problems, any true equation that matches the story is correct (for example, 5 + 7 = 12 or 12 − 5 = 7 when a part is missing); the answer number must match. In fact families, the four facts may be written in any order.
