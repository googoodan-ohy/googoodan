Day 31 · Order Numbers

Unit 4: Skip Count and Compare

Answers are numbered in the same order as the problems on each page (left to right, top to bottom). A square may also be called a rectangle or a quadrilateral, and a rectangle a quadrilateral (the key shows the best name). Accept any correct equation for a word problem (for example 8 + 7 = 15 or 15 − 8 = 7 for a missing-part story); addends may be in either order, and in expanded form a place with no tens or ones gets 0 in its box (405 = 400 + 0 + 5). Times may be written 3:05 or 3:05 a.m./p.m.; equal-share names may be singular (a half, a third, a fourth).
