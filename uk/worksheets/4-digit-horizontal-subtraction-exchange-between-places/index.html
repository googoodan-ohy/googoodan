Teaching Notes

Practice focus: subtraction worksheets borrowing 4 digits. Focus: Four-Digit Subtraction. Use the worked example and teaching sequence below to explain the reasoning before independent practice.

Before You Begin

  • Read the numbers and identify the operation.

Teaching Steps for Parents and Teachers

  1. Start in the ones place and subtract corresponding places.
  2. If a top digit is too small, exchange one unit from the next place for ten units in the current place.
  3. Mark both changes: the giving place decreases by one and the receiving place increases by ten.
  4. Continue left, using the changed digits.

Worked Examples

Evaluate 5002-1786.

  1. Regroup from place value 1,000; it decreases by 1. Each intervening zero becomes 9. There are now 12 units at place value 1.
  2. Place value 1: 12 − 6 = 6.
  3. Place value 10: 9 − 8 = 1.
  4. Place value 100: 9 − 7 = 2.
  5. Place value 1,000: 4 − 1 = 3.

Answer: 3216

Common Mistakes and How to Help

Do not reverse the digits just because the lower digit is larger. Subtraction keeps its original order. When a zero is involved, follow the exchange across the places rather than changing every zero independently.

Add the difference to the number subtracted. You should recover the starting number.

Questions to Check Understanding

  • Add the difference to the number subtracted. You should recover the starting number.
  • Which step explains your answer?
  • Can you apply the same reasoning when the numbers change?

Practice Advice

Work through the example together. Ask the learner to explain the first two problems, then try three independently. Review the first incorrect step before assigning more practice.
