Teaching Notes

Focus: Decimal Place Value. Use the worked example and teaching sequence below to explain the reasoning before independent practice.

Before You Begin

  • Know tenths, hundredths, and thousandths.

Teaching Steps for Parents and Teachers

  1. Locate the decimal point.
  2. Read each digit by its place.
  3. Use a fraction with denominator 10, 100, or 1,000 to describe the decimal part.
  4. For a blank, identify the missing quantity. Substitute the completed value back into the original statement to check it.

Worked Examples

What is the value of 7 in 0.375?

  1. The 7 is in the hundredths place.
  2. 7 hundredths = 7/100.

Answer: 0.07

Common Mistakes and How to Help

A digit farther right has a smaller place value.

Does 0.3 + 0.07 + 0.005 equal 0.375?

Questions to Check Understanding

  • Does 0.3 + 0.07 + 0.005 equal 0.375?
  • Which step explains your answer?
  • Can you apply the same reasoning when the numbers change?

Practice Advice

Work through the example together. Ask the learner to explain the first two problems, then try three independently. Review the first incorrect step before assigning more practice.

Next Practice
