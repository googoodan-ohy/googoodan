Teaching Notes

Use the worked example and teaching sequence below to explain the reasoning before independent practice.

Before You Begin

  • Recognize equal measurement units.

Teaching Steps for Parents and Teachers

  1. Align the starting points when comparing lengths.
  2. Read the scale interval and measure from zero, or subtract the starting reading.
  3. State the length with its unit.

Worked Examples

A segment begins at 2 cm and ends at 7 cm. Find its length.

  1. Subtract the starting reading from the ending reading.
  2. 7 − 2 = 5.

Answer: 5 cm

Common Mistakes and How to Help

An ending ruler reading is not the length unless the object starts at zero.

Can you count five 1-cm intervals?

Questions to Check Understanding

  • Can you count five 1-cm intervals?
  • What in the numbers or diagram supports your answer?

Practice Advice

Work through the example together. Ask the learner to explain the first two problems, then try three independently. Review the first incorrect step before assigning more practice.
