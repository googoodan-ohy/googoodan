Teaching Notes

Focus: Simplest Form. Use the worked example and teaching sequence below to explain the reasoning before independent practice.

Before You Begin

  • Find common factors.

Teaching Steps for Parents and Teachers

  1. Find a common factor of the numerator and denominator.
  2. Divide both by that factor.
  3. Continue until their only common factor is 1.

Worked Examples

Simplify 12/18.

  1. The greatest common factor of 12 and 18 is 6.
  2. 12 ÷ 6 = 2; 18 ÷ 6 = 3.

Answer: 2/3

Common Mistakes and How to Help

Divide both parts by the same factor.

Can the numerator and denominator be divided by another common factor greater than 1?

Questions to Check Understanding

  • Can the numerator and denominator be divided by another common factor greater than 1?
  • Which step explains your answer?
  • Can you apply the same reasoning when the numbers change?

Practice Advice

Work through the example together. Ask the learner to explain the first two problems, then try three independently. Review the first incorrect step before assigning more practice.
