Teaching Notes

Use the worked example and teaching sequence below to explain the reasoning before independent practice.

Before You Begin

  • Recognize a vertex and two rays.

Teaching Steps for Parents and Teachers

  1. Put the protractor center on the vertex.
  2. Align one ray with the zero line.
  3. Read the scale starting at that zero, or mark the required measure and draw the second ray.

Worked Examples

Two adjacent angles measure 35° and 25°. Find their combined angle.

  1. The adjacent angles do not overlap.
  2. Add their measures: 35 + 25 = 60.

Answer: 60°

Common Mistakes and How to Help

Start from the correct zero on the protractor; do not choose the other scale.

Is the result acute, right, or obtuse, and does that fit the drawing?

Questions to Check Understanding

  • Is the result acute, right, or obtuse, and does that fit the drawing?
  • What in the numbers or diagram supports your answer?

Practice Advice

Work through the example together. Ask the learner to explain the first two problems, then try three independently. Review the first incorrect step before assigning more practice.
