정사각형과 직사각형의 관계

풀이 방법을 생각하고 확인하세요.

이름:날짜: ______________
1

Explain why every rectangle is a parallelogram.

예시 / 확인: Both pairs of opposite sides of a rectangle are parallel.
2

Is every parallelogram a rectangle? Give a counterexample.

예시 / 확인: No. A slanted parallelogram without right angles is a counterexample.
3

Name the broadest family containing squares, rectangles, and rhombuses: triangles or quadrilaterals?

예시 / 확인: Quadrilaterals. Each has four sides.
4

A quadrilateral has four equal sides and four right angles. Name its most specific family.

예시 / 확인: Square.
5

A quadrilateral has four right angles and unequal adjacent side lengths. Is it a square?

예시 / 확인: No. It is a rectangle, but its four sides are not all equal.
6

A quadrilateral has four equal sides but no right 학습. Is it a rhombus, a square, or both?

예시 / 확인: A rhombus only. A square must also have four right angles.
7

정사각형, 직사각형, 사각형을 포함 관계에 맞게 좁은 범위부터 쓰세요.

예시 / 확인: Square, rectangle, quadrilateral.
8

Order these families from most specific to broadest: parallelogram, square, rhombus.

예시 / 확인: Square, rhombus, parallelogram.
9

Complete the hierarchy: square is a type of rectangle, and rectangle is a type of _____.

예시 / 확인: Parallelogram (also quadrilateral).
10

Which property do all rectangles share: four right angles or four equal sides?

예시 / 확인: Four right angles. Four equal sides are not required.
