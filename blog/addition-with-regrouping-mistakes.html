How to Help a Child With 2 Digit Addition With Regrouping

A child who knows single-digit facts can still freeze when the ones column makes ten. The difficulty is often not the addition—it is knowing what the extra ten is supposed to do.

During homework, Maya writes 8 + 7 = 15 and stops. The two digits are correct, but she does not know why the 1 must move to the tens column. A place-value picture gives that small 1 a job: it is one new group of ten, not a mark to remember.

Make the new ten visible

Use ten small counters or draw circles. When the ones make ten or more, circle a group of ten and move it to the tens place. Then write the tens and ones separately before combining them.

Practice one kind of question at a time

Start with one regrouping before using two. Ask the child to point to the column where the new ten came from. A fresh printable sheet keeps the pattern familiar while the numbers change.
