How Kids Can Choose Addition or Subtraction in Word Problems

Children often know how to calculate but choose the wrong operation because the story is moving faster than their understanding.

Sofia reads a story about seven birds and three more birds arriving. She reaches for subtraction because she notices two numbers. A quick drawing slows the story down: the group is growing, so addition fits.

Draw before choosing a sign

Ask the child to sketch the starting group, then show what changed. More joining means add; some leaving means subtract. The picture should come before the equation.

Explain the choice out loud

A child who can say “the group got bigger” is less likely to guess from a keyword alone. Use one story at a time, then print a fresh set.
