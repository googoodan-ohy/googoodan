Free maths practice for home or the classroom. Print this Shape Composition Worksheet: Join Pieces without Gaps and its answer key on A4 paper, one page each.

Teaching Notes

Use the worked example and teaching sequence below to explain the reasoning before independent practice.

Before You Begin

  • Recognize simple two-dimensional shapes.

Teaching Steps for Parents and Teachers

  1. Identify the pieces and the target shape.
  2. Rotate or move the pieces so their edges fit.
  3. Check that the completed shape has no gaps or overlaps.

Worked Examples

Can two congruent right triangles cut from a rectangle make the original rectangle?

  1. Place the cut edges together.
  2. Match the outer corners and sides.

Answer: Yes; join the matching diagonal edges.

Common Mistakes and How to Help

Do not count an overlap as part of a valid tiling.

Do the pieces cover the entire target exactly once?

Questions to Check Understanding

  • Do the pieces cover the entire target exactly once?
  • What in the numbers or diagram supports your answer?

Practice Advice

Work through the example together. Ask the learner to explain the first two problems, then try three independently. Review the first incorrect step before assigning more practice.
