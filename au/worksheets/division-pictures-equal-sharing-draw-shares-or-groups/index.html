Teaching Notes

Focus: Sharing Equally. Use the worked example and teaching sequence below to explain the reasoning before independent practice.

Before You Begin

  • Understand equal groups.

Teaching Steps for Parents and Teachers

  1. Identify the total.
  2. Decide whether you need the number in each group or the number of groups.
  3. Share or group equally, then check by multiplication.

Worked Examples

Share 12 counters equally among 3 groups.

  1. Place one counter in each group repeatedly.
  2. Each group gets 4 counters.
  3. 3 × 4 = 12.

Answer: 4 counters in each group

Common Mistakes and How to Help

Do not confuse the number of groups with the size of a group.

Do all groups have the same number, and do they total 12?

Questions to Check Understanding

  • Do all groups have the same number, and do they total 12?
  • Which step explains your answer?
  • Can you apply the same reasoning when the numbers change?

Practice Advice

Work through the example together. Ask the learner to explain the first two problems, then try three independently. Review the first incorrect step before assigning more practice.

Next Practice

  • If the learner needs help, return to the example and model each step before trying again.
